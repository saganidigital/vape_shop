Knowledge Base

Safety, Pod Care & FAQ

Everything you need to know about battery care, pod maintenance, and e-liquid storage.

Store bottles in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures. Nicotine and delicate flavor compounds degrade under UV exposure. Always ensure the child-resistant cap is tightly sealed after each refill.
Nicotine salts have a lower pH balance, allowing for higher nicotine concentrations (e.g. 25mg - 50mg) with a silky-smooth throat hit, making them ideal for compact pod devices. Freebase nicotine provides a more pronounced throat hit at lower strengths (e.g. 3mg - 6mg) and is best suited for high-wattage sub-ohm cloud chasing.
Each Nexus Pro pod lasts between 10 to 14 days under average vaping conditions (or approximately 4,500 puffs). If you notice a reduction in vapor density or a subtle change in flavor clarity, it's time to replace the pod.
Aura devices feature integrated overcharge, overcurrent, and short-circuit protection chips. However, for maximum lithium-ion battery longevity, we recommend disconnecting the device once the smart OLED screen indicates 100% charge (typically under 35 minutes via Type-C).
